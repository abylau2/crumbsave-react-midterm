# CrumbSave visual direction

CrumbSave is an Almaty demo marketplace for end-of-day surprise food bags. The primary action is finding and reserving a bag. It must feel like a food editorial with the utility of a marketplace.

- Direction: warm minimal editorial marketplace. One strong food photograph leads; the catalog is a compact, practical product surface.
- Color: only the brief's forest `#183D32`, ivory `#F7F6F1`, white, sage `#E8EDDF`, pistachio `#DDECB8`, terracotta `#BF684B`, charcoal `#202820`, muted `#6C786F`, and border `#DFE4D9`.
- Type: locally hosted DM Sans for the interface and Newsreader Italic for a few editorial words and numerals. Catalog headings and shopping controls stay in the sans face. Both fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License.
- Layout: asymmetrical hero followed immediately by the catalog, then a short numbered process strip. Search, category navigation, and refinements share thin rules; the three-column catalog is the primary product surface.
- Shape: 8px filter controls, 13px listing cards, 24px hero image. Borders carry the card structure; hover changes the border and image crop without lifting every card.
- Motion: only short state and hover feedback, about 180ms; respect reduced motion.
- Trust: the footer identifies the student project, example places and offers, and the absence of real orders. Restaurant and cafe photographs illustrate food categories without implying a partnership with the named listings.
